What we actually build

Two things. That's on purpose — we'd rather do two things properly than sell you a platform.

  • A site that looks like your company. Home, services, service area, about, and an obvious way to reach you.
  • Your photos, not stock. Your trucks, your crews, your finished work. License and insured line. Real hours.
  • Click-to-call first. A form too, if you'd rather have leads in an inbox than a voicemail.
  • Fast on a cheap phone. Most of your customers are standing in a driveway on bad signal.
  • Service-area pages. So the next town over can find you, not just your home city.

And if you want it — job tools

Only if you ask. Most shops don't need this on day one.

  • Jobs and lots, with the crew assigned to each
  • Photos from the field, attached to the job
  • Status that means something: scheduled, in progress, done
  • Your words. If you say tickets, it says tickets.

Not a second Procore. A board your foreman will actually open on his phone.
